# Sudo Privacy Interaction & Mitigation Android SDK

## Overview

The Sudo Privacy Interaction & Mitigation SDK allows you to offer your users' the ability to securely connect their online virtual presence — such as an email address — and discover the organizations with which that presence has a relationship. Discovered organizations are analysed for privacy practices, data breaches, and overall good-citizen behaviour. The SDK surfaces these results along with recommendations for an appropriate course of action to sanitise your user's digital footprint. The Sudo Platform makes it effortless to integrate privacy interaction and mitigation capabilities within new or existing products.

Please see the [Sudo Platform Developer Docs](https://docs.sudoplatform.com/guides/digital-account-protection) for an overview of Privacy Interaction in the Sudo Platform.
